Recommended tutorial

Your first reproducible design.

Start with a built-in design, inspect the generated circuit, and then move it through physical implementation.

  1. 1
    Choose a frontend input

    Use an audited example, upload Verilog/SystemVerilog, or begin a natural-language specification session.

  2. 2
    Review the circuit

    Check gate count, synthesized schematic, RTL source, and gate-level netlist before implementation.

  3. 3
    Configure the physical flow

    Select PDK-facing constraints, clock, utilization, density, target stage, and optimization mode.

  4. 4
    Inspect and reuse the evidence

    Review layout, QoR, reports, artifacts, optimization history, and verified learning records.
